Competition Rules

Important
  • 01
    Maximum Daily Loss5% of the fixed $50,000 account ($2,500). Checked across the trading day, including combined trade losses recorded in reports.
  • 02
    Maximum Overall LossFixed at 10% of $50,000. Equity/balance must not reach $45,000 or below.
  • 03
    Inactivity Rule5 consecutive inactive calendar days. A demo account may be automatically deleted after 1 week of inactivity.
  • 04
    Layering RuleMaximum 3 simultaneous open positions across the account. Opening a 4th overlapping position is a breach.
  • 05
    Minimum Profit for Prize EligibilityA trader must achieve at least 3% profit/return and have zero rule breaches to qualify for the 1st prize. Any trader who breaches a rule is moved to the bottom of the leaderboard and is not eligible for the prize, regardless of balance.
  • 06
    Winner Selection at Competition EndAt the end of the competition, the 1st-prize winner is the eligible trader with the highest ending account balance. A trader must meet the 3% minimum return and follow every competition rule; any rule breach disqualifies them from the prize.
